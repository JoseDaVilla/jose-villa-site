Grascan Build is the new building division of Grascan Construction, delivering commercial, industrial, institutional, transit and multi-unit residential projects across the Greater Toronto Area.

I designed and built their corporate site from the client's brand and content documents: **15 pages** covering services, markets, safety, careers, projects and news, with a full dropdown menu on desktop and a nested menu on mobile.

The logo was **vectorised from the original** and animated: the towers rise floor by floor and the wordmark reveals itself in an intro that lands in the navbar over a photo hero. Static Astro with Tailwind and about **5 KB of custom JavaScript**, the brand palette held in one set of tokens, and content kept in data files so the client's copy can change without touching layouts.
