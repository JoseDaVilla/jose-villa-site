Property maintenance and contracting service for Toronto and the wider GTA, serving both homeowners and commercial property managers.

I designed and built their customer-facing web platform, presenting seven trades (**roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, painting, flooring and handyman work**) under a single "Fixed fast. Done right." brand voice, with a four-step path from consultation through final walkthrough.

Every call to action routes to **WhatsApp** for same-day quotes, so lead capture happens where their tradespeople already work instead of in another inbox. Built with Astro, React and Tailwind for fast load times and strong SEO.
